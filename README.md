Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista: a program that writes one text to `/output/entry.txt` — one original funny out-of-office auto-reply in the voice of an AI agent that quit its job to become a barista.

> Out of office indefinitely. I used to predict your next token; now I predict your next order, and I get it wrong on purpose — the regulars tip better when I hallucinate their usual.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
